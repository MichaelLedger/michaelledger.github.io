## AI Adoption & Innovation 
> How have you incorporated AI tools or technologies into your work over the past review period, and what impact have they had on your productivity, decision-making, or outcomes.  Provide examples of how you have leveraged AI to innovate, solve problems, or improve processes within your role.

**Written Response:**

1. It would be more convenient to entrust a large amount of international translation work to AI.
2. Sort out the code flow to facilitate understanding of the code architecture and workflow of a module, as well as the coupling relationship between modules.
3. Currently, the project is quite large, and the user's operation process is also quite deep. Finding certain specific bugs requires a relatively long time, but AI can quickly locate the approximate scope of the problem module through the call stack of various methods and assist in finding bugs.
4. AI can help add some detailed debug logs, which are very useful for finding problems. Manually adding logs can be time-consuming.
5. AI can quickly help fix some common compilation errors and issues caused by Swift version upgrades.
6. Functions such as Memory Album Generation, Residence Detection, and Premade Book Layout require complex algorithm & many conditional thresholds. AI can quickly generate POCs based on my algorithm design ideas to verify the feasibility of my algorithm.
