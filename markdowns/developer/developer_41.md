# Mac Calendar: Lunar dates & festival holidays

A short guide for showing **lunar dates** in Apple Calendar, then **subscribing** to China’s public and traditional holidays (including major lunar festivals that are also rest days).

---

## 1. Turn on the built-in lunar calendar

This shows lunar dates **next to** Gregorian dates. It does **not** add festival events by itself.

1. Open the **Calendar** app on your Mac.
2. In the menu bar, choose **Calendar → Settings…** (or **Preferences…** on older macOS).
3. Open the **General** tab.
4. Check **Show alternate calendar**.
5. Pick the lunar calendar you want:
   - **Chinese**
   - **Hebrew**
   - **Islamic**

Lunar dates should now appear alongside the usual Gregorian dates in month and day views.

---

## 2. Subscribe to Chinese public & traditional holidays

Use a calendar **subscription** (`.ics`) so holidays and major lunar festivals show up as events.

1. Open the **Calendar** app.
2. In the menu bar, choose **File → New Calendar Subscription…**
3. Paste the link below, then continue.
4. Give the calendar a clear name (for example: **China Holidays**).
5. Choose how often it should refresh (weekly is usually enough).
6. Click **OK** / **Subscribe**.

### Subscription link

**Official China public & traditional holidays** (Office Holidays) — includes major lunar festivals that are also national rest days:

```
https://www.officeholidays.com/ics/china
```

Copy that URL, then paste it into **New Calendar Subscription**.

---

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Alternate calendar enabled (Chinese lunar if that’s what you want)
- [ ] Subscribed to `https://www.officeholidays.com/ics/china`
- [ ] Calendar appears in the left sidebar and is checked so events show

---

## Tips

- **Dates vs events:** the built-in lunar option is for *dates on the grid*. The `.ics` subscription is for *holiday events* (Spring Festival, National Day, and other listed days).
- **Location:** Office Holidays’ China feed is aimed at **PRC public holidays**, not every regional or folk observance.
- **Refresh:** if a holiday date looks stale, select the subscribed calendar, open its info, and refresh or bump the auto-refresh interval.
- **Privacy:** a subscription is a live URL; Calendar will fetch updates from Office Holidays on the schedule you chose.

Enjoy a calendar that shows both the lunar date and the festival days that actually get a day off.
